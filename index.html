Jab Main Ek Student Tha, Tab Mera Wajan 87.4 KG Tha...

Student life mein badhte wajan (87.4 KG) ki wajah se mujhe rozmarra ki zindagi mein bahut pareshaniyon ka saamna karna padta tha. Thoda sa chalne-phirne mein dikkat hoti thi, aur kai health problems ne gher liya tha — jaise gas aur acidity ki samasya, har waqt weakness aur thakawat (fatigue), aur thodi si seedhiyan chadhne ya chalne par saans phoolna (breathlessness).

Tab maine samjha ki bhookha rehne ya crash dieting se wajan kam nahi hota, balki body aur kamzor hoti hai. Maine scientific Calorie Deficit, ghar ke santulit khane aur World's #1 Nutrition ke support se apna 17.2 KG wajan kam kiya (87.4 KG se 70.2 KG).

Jab mera gas, thakawat aur saans phoolne ka problem poori tarah theek hua aur main fit aur energetic mehsoos karne laga, toh maine ise apna Life Mission bana liya! Aaj mera mission hai zyada se zyada logon ko स्वस्थ (healthy) aur fit banana, aur ghar baithe logon ki help karna — taaki har parivar bina kisi tension ke sehatmand zindagi ji sake.
